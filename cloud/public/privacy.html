Nova Clip

Privacy

Nova Clip is a game clipping app for Windows. This page covers its online part, where you can send clips from your PC and watch them on your phone.

What's stored

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, profile picture and Google's account ID. Nothing else; Nova Clip never sees your Google password.
  • The clips you choose to send, with their names, length, game and a preview picture.
  • A sign-in key for each phone or PC you've signed in on.

Who can see it

Only you, when signed in with the same Google account. Clips aren't public and aren't shared with anyone. They're stored on Cloudflare, which runs the service.

Deleting things

Delete a clip on the phone page and it's gone from the service. When your space is full, your oldest clips are deleted to make room (they stay on your PC). Signing out of Nova Clip on your PC removes that PC's sign-in key. To have everything about you removed, email imyoursonhomer@gmail.com.
